# VoiceBridge AI

## Customer intent, carried through.

Customers describe what they need in everyday language. Sales teams need timely, accurate answers; product managers need clear scope; engineers need testable requirements. During those handoffs, customer constraints can disappear and assumptions can become promises.

VoiceBridge AI connects the conversation from first question to customer response. Its sales copilot answers from approved product sources and shows its evidence. The workspace turns conversation statements into traceable requirements, lets product and engineering reviewers clarify scope and feasibility, and checks a proposed response against the customer’s original words before approval. Teams can also practise customer conversations and review evidence-linked communication signals.

The demonstration follows a marketplace operator asking how to recommend new products with little or no interaction history. VoiceBridge surfaces the concern, preserves it in the requirements, carries data and evaluation questions into engineering review, and helps prepare a qualified response. Human reviewers approve requirements, technical assessments and customer-facing commitments.

The prototype integrates Gnani Prisma for speech transcription and Gnani Timbre for generated speech. Local development uses Ollama with Qwen 3 4B for reasoning. Conversation examples are synthetic. Model suggestions are reviewable; they do not replace technical validation or human approval.
